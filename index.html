Yes. Pick items from the catalogue, or let us put together a best-sellers sample package. The sample fee is credited toward your first order.

Yes — logo on the product, your own labels and tags, or a full packaging change. Each route has a different minimum and cost, so tell us what you're planning and roughly how many pieces, and we'll work out an exact quote.

Are your products certified?

Our factory is ISO 9001 certified, and products carry CE, RoHS and FCC certifications suitable for mainstream sales channels in most markets.

Shipping is charged at actual cost and depends on weight and destination. Express takes about 5–8 days to the US, air freight 10–15 days, and sea freight 25–35 days for bulk. Send us your shortlist and we'll quote the exact shipping for each option before you decide.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or Wise. We confirm the total — items plus shipping — before you pay anything.

Is the packaging in English?

Yes. Products come with English packaging and English manuals as standard.
